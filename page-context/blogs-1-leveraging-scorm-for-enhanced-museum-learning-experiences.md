### Abstract

- **Content type:** Content section
- **Heading:** Abstract
- **Paragraph:**

  This discourse examines the implementation of Sharable Content Object Reference Model (SCORM) standards in the creation of digital educational content for museums. By adhering to SCORM guidelines, Museoedu enhances the interoperability, accessibility, and effectiveness of e-learning modules across various learning management systems (LMS). This alignment with best practices promotes continuous education and aligns with the recommendations of the American Alliance of Museums (AAM) and the International Council of Museums (ICOM).

### Introduction

- **Content type:** Content section
- **Heading:** Introduction
- **Paragraph:**

  As museums increasingly adopt digital tools for education, the standardization of e-learning content becomes crucial. SCORM, a set of technical standards for e-learning products, ensures that digital learning materials are effective and accessible on multiple platforms. Museoedu's commitment to SCORM standards exemplifies its dedication to providing high-quality, accessible educational resources in line with industry best practices.
